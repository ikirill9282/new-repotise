I. Управление Аккаунтом Пользователя
Email:
1. Название: Welcome to TrekGuider! Let's Confirm Your Email
   * Триггер: Новый пользователь успешно ввел данные для регистрации.
   * Получатель: Новый Пользователь (Покупатель/Продавец).
   * Тип: Email
   * Цель: Приветствие, предоставление ссылки для верификации email адреса, базовая информация.
   * Body: 
Hi there,
Welcome to TrekGuider! We're thrilled you've joined our community of travel explorers.
Just one more step to get started: please confirm your email address by clicking the button below:
[Verify Your Email Address] (Button)
Didn't sign up for TrekGuider? No worries, you can safely ignore this email.
Adventure awaits,
The TrekGuider Crew
2. Название: Your TrekGuider Password Reset Code
   * Триггер: Пользователь запросил сброс пароля на сайте и ожидает код.
   * Получатель: Пользователь.
   * Тип: Email
   * Цель: Доставить код для ввода на странице сброса пароля.
   * Body:
Hi {User_Name_Placeholder},
We received a request to reset the password for your TrekGuider account.
Here's your code to set a new password:
{Six_Digit_Code_Placeholder}
This code will expire in 1 hour for your security.
If you didn't request this, you can safely ignore this email. If you have any security concerns, please reach out to our support team.
Best,
The TrekGuider Team
3. Название: Your TrekGuider Email Address Has Been Updated
   * Триггер: Пользователь успешно изменил свой email адрес (после подтверждения нового, если требуется).
   * Получатель: Пользователь (на старый и новый email).
   * Тип: Email
   * Цель: Уведомление об изменении email (безопасность).
   * Title (for new email address): Your TrekGuider Email Address Has Been Updated
   * Body (for new email address):
Hi {User_Name_Placeholder},
Just confirming: the email address for your TrekGuider account has been successfully updated to this one.
If you didn't make this change, please contact our support team immediately.
All the best,
The TrekGuider Team
   * Title (for old email address): Security Alert: Your TrekGuider Email Was Changed
   * Body (for old email address):
Hi {User_Name_Placeholder},
The email address for your TrekGuider account was recently changed to {New_Email_Address_Placeholder}.
If this was you, no action is needed.
If you didn't make this change, please contact our support team immediately so we can help secure your account.
Stay safe,
The TrekGuider Team
4. Название: Your TrekGuider Account Deletion Code
   * Триггер: Пользователь подтвердил свое намерение удалить аккаунт (ввел пароль) и ожидает код.
   * Получатель: Пользователь.
   * Тип: Email
   * Цель: Доставить код для финального подтверждения удаления аккаунта.
   * Body:
Hi {User_Name_Placeholder},
We've received your request to delete your TrekGuider account. To confirm this action, please use the following code:
{Six_Digit_Code_Placeholder}
Once you confirm with this code, your account and all associated data will be scheduled for permanent deletion in 30 days. You can cancel this deletion by simply logging back into your account anytime within these 30 days.
If you didn't request this, or if you've changed your mind and don't want to use this code, please ignore this email. If you have security concerns, contact our support team immediately.
Best,
The TrekGuider Team
